NC Quality (2024)

NC Quality

was a small business that my best friend and I co-founded as a passion project. Our goal was to build something meaningful together while developing both our professional skills and personal growth. My co-founder, Namol, had recently graduated with a business degree, while I brought experience in creative design, branding, and content creation. After months of researching suppliers, designing products, and building our brand, we launched NC Quality in 2024. The business specialized in premium silk products, including pillowcases, eye masks, and scrunchies, which were sold through our online store. As the Creative Director, I was responsible for shaping the brand's visual identity and online presence. My responsibilities included:
  • Designing the company logo and brand identity
  • Planning and directing product photography
  • Creating visual content for marketing campaigns
  • Managing our TikTok and Instagram accounts
  • Developing social media strategies focused on engaging Gen Z audiences

Social media became one of our strongest marketing channels, allowing us to build brand awareness organically through creative content and a clear understanding of our target audience.

Although we eventually decided to close the business due to time constraints and living in different locations, the experience was incredibly valuable. It strengthened my skills in branding, photography, content creation, and digital marketing, while confirming my passion for design and creative direction. Most importantly, it taught me how to build a brand from the ground up and collaborate effectively as part of a small entrepreneurial team.
